SyncSpace

Co-working rooms for students

Study together, stay in sync.

SyncSpace is a video co-working space with a shared Pomodoro timer. Join friends in a room, focus in rounds, and get a short quiz built from your own notes when each round ends.

  • One timer for the whole room

    Focus, short break and long break stay in step for everyone inside.

  • Temporary or permanent rooms

    Open a one-off session that closes when it ends, or keep a room that is always there for your group.

  • Quizzes from your notes

    Upload notes or PDFs and review them with questions and flashcards after each focus round.
